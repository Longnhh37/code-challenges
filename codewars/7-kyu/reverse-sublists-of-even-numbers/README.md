<h2><a href=https://www.codewars.com/kata/64c7bbad0a2a00198657013d/train/rust target="_blank">Reverse sublists of even numbers</a></h2><h3>7 kyu</h3><p>Write a function <code>revSub</code> which reverses all sublists where consecutive elements are even.Note that the odd numbers should remain where they are.</p><h1 id="example">Example</h1><p>With <code>[1,2,4,5,9]</code> as input, we should get <code>[1,4,2,5,9]</code>.Here, because <code>[2,4]</code> is a sublist of consecutive even numbers, it should be flipped. There could be more than one sublist of even numbers, or none at all.</p><p>A few other examples:</p><ul><li><code>[] -&gt; []</code></li><li><code>[2,4] -&gt; [4,2]</code></li><li><code>[2,4,3] -&gt; [4,2,3]</code></li><li><code>[2,4,3,10,2] -&gt; [4,2,3,2,10]</code></li></ul><p>Have fun coding!</p>
